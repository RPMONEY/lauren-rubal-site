About Dr. Rubal

Deep expertise. A more comprehensive approach.

Lauren Rubal, MD, is a USC-trained Reproductive Endocrinology & Infertility physician with a boutique practice in Orange County, California. Her unique approach focuses on fertility, recurrent miscarriage, painful, irregular or heavy cycles, and menopause through an integrative lens.

Dr. Rubal is double board certified in Integrative Medicine and OB/GYN. She is a FEMM Fellow at the Reproductive Health Research Institute and has completed a fellowship with the Leonine Forum.

A different vision for reproductive care.

One of Dr. Rubal's defining career decisions was leaving a traditional reproductive endocrinology partnership to pursue a radically reimagined vision for reproductive care.

Her mission is to identify the root causes of reproductive issues and help restore health through a holistic, individualized approach. Rather than looking at reproductive symptoms in isolation, she considers the broader picture to better understand what may be contributing to them.

Dr. Rubal is a Board Member of the Couple to Couple League and serves on the Advisory Board for the nonprofit Pre-Health Shadowing.

When she's not at work, she's usually wrangling her three boys with her husband, attempting to homestead, or spending time in nature. As a native Floridian, just usually not in the water.
